# Disqualification (B-out)

Disqualifying a shooter removes them from the match: all their stage scores are set to zero, and they appear in the DQ list in the results.

## How to DQ a shooter

In the scoring sheet, scroll to the bottom and click the **DQ** button.

![DQ confirm modal](/docs/screenshots/dq-confirm-modal.png)

Pick a reason from the dropdown and confirm. The shooter row in the registration table turns red and gets a "DQ" badge. To undo, click **Remove DQ** in the same place on the scoring sheet.

## Pre-defined reasons

The DQ modal offers a list of pre-defined reasons (rule violations, safety, etc.). The stored value is the translated text of the chosen reason, displayed everywhere the DQ is referenced.

## Effect on results

- All stage scores for the shooter are set to zero.
- The shooter is added to the DQ table at the bottom of every results view (by division, overall, by stage, by category, by tag).
- The DQ reason is shown next to the shooter's name in the DQ tables.

![Results DQ table](/docs/screenshots/results-dq-table.png)

## Who can DQ

Anyone with access to a match can DQ — there is no admin gate on this action. If you need an admin-only DQ, speak to the host.

**To do this in the app:** [Go to scoring](app-tab:scoring)
